The **Appearance** tab of **Settings** sets the colors of the app.
Every account sees it, the Owner included, in the browser and in the desktop app.

Every change saves as it is made.
The choice is stored in the browser or desktop app, not in the account, so a second browser has its own.

## Theme

Two cards, **Light** and **Dark**, choose the mode.
Each card previews its header and accent color.
A new browser starts in **Dark**.

**Match your device theme** follows the light or dark setting of the operating system.
Choosing a card clears the checkbox.

## Colors

A theme is four colors:

- **Light header**
- **Light accent**
- **Dark header**
- **Dark accent**

Each row has a color picker and a field that takes a hex code, such as `#2d8b8b`.

## Share theme

**Share theme** holds the four color codes as one line, separated by commas, in the order above.
The default reads `#f1faee,#2d8b8b,#1a2332,#a8dadc`.

**Copy** puts the line on the clipboard.
Pasting another line into the field and leaving it applies that theme.
A line that is not four valid color codes is refused with "Enter four valid color codes." and the field returns to the current theme.

## Tried and true

**Tried and true** offers 13 built-in themes as round swatches.
Each swatch shows the theme's four colors, and its name appears on hover.
Selecting one replaces the four colors.

The themes are Ocean Depths, Graphite Blue, Light, Dark, Sunset Boulevard, Forest Canopy, Modern Minimalist, Golden Hour, Arctic Frost, Desert Rose, Tech Innovation, Botanical Garden, and Midnight Galaxy.
Ocean Depths is the default.
